# 🎄 Merry, Merry Christmas ✨

A pink, sparkly Christmas tree made of thousands of glowing particles that you control with your hand, all to a music-box carol 🎶

### 👉 [Open the tree](https://olivia-li-00.github.io/vibe_christmas_tree/)

## How to play

| Gesture | What happens |
|---|---|
| ✋ **Open hand** | The tree scatters into the sky |
| ✊ **Fist** | The lights gather into a glowing heart |
| ☝️ **Point and twirl** | A ribbon of white-gold stars winds up the tree, following the way you circle |
| 🎄 **Hand away** | The tree comes back together |

**No camera?** Hold **E** to scatter, **C** to gather, and **← / →** to wind the ribbon.
Drag to look around, scroll to zoom, and tap **♪** to turn the music on or off.

> Best in Chrome on a laptop. Your camera video stays on your device and is never uploaded.

## Made with

[Three.js](https://threejs.org/) for the sparkles · [MediaPipe](https://developers.google.com/mediapipe) for hand tracking · a traditional English carol played by a little in-browser music box 💕
